Why the middle column differs: the admin decides to proceed or cancel. Three facts matter: deleted users exist, how many, and who still gets the work. Names of deleted users do not change that decision and do not scale. So the middle modals state a count and list the names in a box that scrolls after 3 rows. No cap, no "and N more", nothing hidden.
Same in every count: the middle title ("Deleted assignees won't be assigned") and the count sentence ("N of M assignees are deleted users"). Only the numbers change.
Plural forms: "the other assignee" when 1 remains  |  "the other N assignees" when 2 or more remain  |  "1 of 3 assignees is a deleted user" in the banner, "2 of 3 assignees are deleted users" for 2 or more
To verify before use: "Someone can be assigned after it's created" assumes an unassigned work order can be assigned later; not tested. The End date form frame was not found, so the banner stays in the details panel. The scrolling list is new and is not in Figma; the designer must confirm the 3-row height and whether a scroll box fits the modal pattern. The 5575 banner already lists all names in Assigned To, so it needs no list.
